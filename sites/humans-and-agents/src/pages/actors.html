Background · interests

Actors

The big players made the agent transport protocols neutral, then moved up a layer to compete on identity, registries, and verification. That is where lock-in came back for email.

Evidence current to 18 October 2026

SMTP stayed open, and Gmail and Outlook ended up setting email's rules anyway. Agents are heading the same way. MCP and A2A sit in a neutral foundation, so the fight is over who verifies agents, who keeps the list of real ones, and who sits in front of the systems agents need to reach.

The verifiers are consolidating

Identity companies are being bought, and the buyers cite agents by name. Palo Alto Networks closed its purchase of CyberArk, about $25B, in February 2026. ServiceNow paid about $1.2B for Veza. CrowdStrike bought SGNL for about $740M, its chief executive calling "every agent a privileged identity". Okta bought Axiom and Permiso. The number of independent identity verifiers is shrinking.

Three conflicts to watch

Whose identity is the agent?

The consumer account ("my ChatGPT") or the employer's directory ("Acme's agent, acting for Priya")? ID-JAG puts the employer in charge at work, but only where the employer routes agents through enterprise sign-in. Workers at more than 90% of companies use personal AI tools for work, while only 40% of companies buy an official subscription. For consumers, the Personal Agent Protocol answers one way: the agent belongs to the person, and the company decides what it may do after the person signs in. At work the question is open, and everyday use is tilting it toward the personal account the employer never sees.

The toll on reading

This is now a default setting rather than a forecast. Since 15 September 2026, new Cloudflare domains that show ads block "Agent" fetchers by default, and Cloudflare counts ChatGPT-User as an agent. Cloudflare sits in front of 27.6% of all websites, and it is moving from charging per crawl to paying publishers per use in answers. For business, that means a link sent to a partner, or a vendor's documentation an agent needs, can be invisible to the agent because of a default neither company chose.

Open transport, closed registries

Half of this is settled. Transport is neutral, and enforcement is concentrated in a few edges that verify the same Web Bot Auth signatures. Registries are still scattered across edge directories, identity providers' agent directories, and payment schemes such as Visa's Trusted Agent Protocol and Mastercard Agent Pay. Whoever consolidates the registry plays Gmail's role.

Revenue tells a different story. Okta said in May 2026 that its AI-agent products were "not materially contributing". Cloudflare is the clearest earner among the brokers, with $696.1M of revenue in the second quarter of 2026, up 36%, but it sells delivery and verification together, so its numbers can't separate the two.

Email offers the cleanest precedent. Proofpoint, which filters mail, passed $1B in revenue in 2020 and was taken private for $12.3B. SendGrid, the largest email-sending service, had $111.9M of revenue in 2017 while sending 450B emails. The filter was worth far more than the pipe.
